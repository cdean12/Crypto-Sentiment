How well has sentiment predicted price?

Level correlation: does a higher score line up with higher returns over the next N days (+1 yes, 0 no link, -1 opposite)? Change correlation does the same for the 7-day change in the score. Hit rate: share of days where a non-neutral score pointed the same way as the next move. Compare it with the base rate of price simply rising; a score is only useful if it beats that. A negative correlation means the crowd has been a contrarian signal. Windows overlap, so results look more certain than they are. Treat anything under ~100 observations as noise.
